# Bengal Press & Media World Ltd

Corporate publication and newspaper conglomerate portal for **Bengal Press & Media World Ltd**, modeled after the structure, architecture, and luxury corporate feel of **[Transcom BD](https://www.transcombd.com/)**.

## Live Preview
- **GitHub Pages:** [https://amanurrahman101.github.io/Bengal-Media-Website/](https://amanurrahman101.github.io/Bengal-Media-Website/)

---

## Key Highlights
- **100% Real-World Photography:** High-resolution genuine photography of industrial Goss web offset presses, active newsrooms, broadcast studios, and photojournalism (no synthetic AI artifacts).
- **Transcom Minimalist Aesthetic:** Full-bleed 100vh hero slider with bold uppercase typography, segmented progress bars, slide counters (`01 / 04`), and floating glassmorphism header.
- **Editorial & Print Excellence:**
  - *Daily Bengal (দৈনিক বেঙ্গল)* — Flagship broadsheet (450,000+ daily copies).
  - *The Bengal Herald* — English broadsheet & diplomatic edition.
  - *Bengal Digital Network* — 24/7 breaking news reaching 42M+ monthly readers.
  - *Gazipur Commercial Web Offset Press* — 180,000 impressions/hr industrial facility with FSC paper and organic soy inks.
  - *Bengal Business Review* — Weekly macroeconomic journal.
  - *Protiddhoni Media Labs* — Investigative documentary and audio podcast arm.
- **Interactive Features:** Slide-out Connect drawer with encrypted news tip submissions, advertising rate inquiries, and commercial print specifications.

## Technology Stack
- Semantic HTML5, CSS3 (Modern Variables, Flexbox, CSS Grid)
- High-Performance Vanilla JavaScript
- Scalable Vector Graphics (SVG)
