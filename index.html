EDITORIAL HERITAGE

A Legacy Built on Truth, Independence & Excellence

“A free and courageous press is the bedrock of democracy. Our responsibility is not to the powerful, but to the truth and the people of Bangladesh.”

Originating as an independent broadsheet publishing house in Dhaka, Bengal Press & Media World Ltd has grown into Bangladesh's foremost integrated news, publishing, and media conglomerate. Through political transitions, technological disruptions, and rapid national development, Bengal Press has remained the steadfast benchmark of credible journalism and ethical business conduct.
