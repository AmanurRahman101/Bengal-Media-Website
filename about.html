OUR ORIGIN & VALUES

The Foundation of Credible Media

Bengal Press & Media World Ltd was founded with a singular conviction: that a nation cannot prosper without an informed, fearless, and independent citizenry. While many media outlets succumbed to commercial and partisan pressures, Bengal Press built its reputation on strict factual corroboration, impartial investigative scrutiny, and cultural celebration.

“Journalism without independence is mere public relations. We hold power to account, defend democratic institutions, and give voice to the disenfranchised.”

Today, through flagship broadsheets, English daily journalism, and real-time digital networks, Bengal Press remains the nation's most trusted chronicler of modern history.

CORE PRINCIPLES

The Bengal Press Editorial Code of Conduct

Our editors and correspondents adhere strictly to the following six constitutional pillars of journalism:

01

Mandatory Multi-Source Corroboration

Every factual assertion requires independent verification from at least two distinct, credible sources before publication.

02

Absolute Commercial Firewall

An impenetrable separation exists between our newsroom and advertising sales. Commercial considerations never influence editorial coverage.

03

Whistleblower & Source Protection

We provide absolute legal, operational, and digital protection to confidential sources who disclose matters of genuine public interest.

04

Transparent Corrections & Fair Hearing

Any factual error is promptly acknowledged with equal prominence. Subjects of investigative reportage are always afforded fair right of reply.

05

Zero Conflicts of Interest

No reporter or editor may accept honoraria, gifts, hospitality, or political appointments that compromise journalistic impartiality.

06

Human Dignity & Privacy

Respecting personal privacy, protection of minors, and ethical restraint during coverage of tragedies, disasters, and court proceedings.
